Money & Vault Systems
MONEY & VAULT SYSTEMS

Family & Vault

  • Per-person tracking: Banks → Deposits
  • Fixed & Recurring Deposits, Insurance, Post Office, Mutual Funds
  • Nominees, top-ups, paused SIPs, extended policy terms
  • Estimated maturity values from the entered terms

Alerts & Warnings

  • Red = act now, yellow = due soon, rolled up family-wide
  • Bell with Today, Tomorrow, 7 Days, Month, Quarter, Missed
  • Mark Done with undo for missed instalments
  • Rotating “Next Up” panel across every category

Dashboards & Reports

  • Charts, calendars and filters for all four money areas
  • Daily Log and Invoice dashboards
  • Excel, CSV and PDF exports through the Share menu

Backup & Restore

  • Password-encrypted backup with a choose-what-to-include list
  • Merge or Replace restore
  • Side-by-side conflict review before anything is saved
Daily Life, Work & Security
DAILY LIFE, WORK & SECURITY

Daily Log

  • Expense register with categories and a month calendar
  • Recurring bills tracked per occurrence
  • Receipt PDF and a reminder schedule

Notes & Stash

  • Coloured sticky notes with rich text
  • Private Stash for passwords and PINs, blurred until revealed
  • Separate PIN-and-pattern lock for the Stash

Work Tools

  • Five-step invoice wizard: timesheet to PDF
  • Rolodex contact book
  • Certificate tracker with folders and expiry reminders

Layered Security

  • App Lock: PIN, swipe code and tap rhythm, one asked at random
  • Optional fingerprint / face unlock
  • Lock-out after repeated wrong tries
  • Lock returns whenever the app is reopened
